# CampusLoop AI — Responsible AI & Ethical Framework

CampusLoop AI adheres strictly to responsible, ethical, and human-centered AI design principles across all operational tiers.

---

## 1. Transparency & Explainability
- **No Black-Box Actions:** Every food waste projection is accompanied by an explicit risk tier reason, threshold boundary references, and policy evidence citations.
- **Clear Operational Labeling:** The system clearly delineates:
  - **Forecast:** Statistical estimate produced by machine learning.
  - **Policy Evidence:** Institutional SOP citations retrieved by RAG.
  - **Measured Outcome:** Post-service empirical scale weight.
  - **Assumption:** Configurable impact parameters (e.g. 20% default reduction scenario).

---

## 2. Human Oversight & Decision Autonomy
- **Decision-Support, Not Replacement:** CampusLoop AI is designed to support campus dining managers and cafeteria chefs, never to automate personnel out of decision-making.
- **Mandatory Approval Gates:** High and Medium risk interventions require explicit human confirmation (`approved_by`) before being scheduled or marked executed.
- **Override Authority:** Dining staff retain complete discretion to reject, adjust, or override any automated recommendation.

---

## 3. Privacy & Data Minimization
- **Zero Student PII:** The platform processes exclusively aggregate operational statistics (expected diners, weather conditions, meal service category, kilograms of food).
- **No Individual Tracking:** Neither student identification numbers, personal meal-swipe card IDs, nor dietary preference records are collected, stored, or processed.

---

## 4. Hallucination Controls for Generative AI (IBM Granite)
- **Zero Numerical Generation by LLM:** Foundation models (IBM Granite) are strictly prohibited from calculating predictions, percentages, or waste weights.
- **Structured Fact Grounding:** Granite is provided immutable numeric facts generated by the Random Forest model and rule engines. Its role is strictly linguistic synthesis: translating numerical facts and institutional SOP citations into concise executive briefings.
- **Transparent Simulation Mode:** When external cloud credentials are absent, the system transparently utilizes local grounded template synthesis, never generating ungrounded facts.

---

## 5. Environmental & Socioeconomic Alignment (UN SDG 12)
- Directly targets **UN SDG Target 12.3**: "By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at the retail and consumer levels and reduce food losses along production and supply chains."
- Grounded in authoritative conversion factors (EPA WARM, FAO Food Wastage Footprint, USDA portion standards) to ensure environmental metrics reflect rigorous scientific benchmarks rather than inflated claims.
