# Campus Organics Diversion & Composting Protocol (ENV-07)

## 1. Zero Waste Hierarchy
In accordance with EPA and UN SDG 12 guidance, campus organics management adheres strictly to the Food Recovery Hierarchy:
1. Source Reduction (Prevent waste through ML forecasting)
2. Feed Hungry People (Donation to campus pantry)
3. Feed Animals (Local agricultural partnerships)
4. Industrial Uses & Anaerobic Digestion
5. Composting (Soil nutrient reclamation)
6. Landfill / Incineration (Last resort to be avoided)

## 2. Composting Standards & Contamination Prevention
- Inevitable food scraps (vegetable trimmings, coffee grounds, eggshells, post-consumer plate waste) must be deposited in dedicated green organics totes.
- Zero contamination policy: No single-use plastics, foil wrappers, or petroleum-based cutlery. Only BPI-certified compostable packaging is permitted.
- Weigh scales at central receiving docks log organic tonnage weekly to track campus diversion rates toward the institution's 2030 Zero-Waste benchmark.
