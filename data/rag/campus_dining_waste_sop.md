# Campus Dining Food Waste Prevention Standard Operating Procedures (SOP-01)

## 1. Scope & Objective
This procedure applies to all campus dining halls, student union cafeterias, and catered institutional events. The primary objective is to eliminate avoidable preparation overages and maintain compliance with UN SDG 12 (Target 12.3: Halving per capita food waste).

## 2. Pre-Service Attendance Forecasting
- Head chefs and dining supervisors must consult the pre-service forecasting intelligence system (CampusLoop AI) at least 4 hours prior to meal service.
- Preparation quantities must be adjusted downward by 10% to 15% when forecasted attendance falls below 80% of nominal capacity (e.g., during inclement weather, Fridays, or post-exam periods).
- Progressive batch cooking (batch preparation of 25-50 portions rather than full-capacity runs) must be utilized for all proteins and hot entrees during the final 90 minutes of any meal service window.

## 3. Plate Waste & Kitchen Overproduction Monitoring
- Stations with historical waste exceeding 10 kg per service period must conduct daily post-service weigh-ins.
- All kitchen prep scraps and unserved batch surplus must be segregated into tare-weighed food collection bins.
- Records of daily waste quantities must be logged into the feedback system within 2 hours of meal service completion.
