# Campus Surplus Food Recovery & Donation Guidelines (POL-04)

## 1. Legal Protection & Compliance
- Food donation is federally protected under the Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act, shielding dining operations from civil and criminal liability when donating wholesome food in good faith to non-profit organizations and student food pantries.
- All campus donations must be logged with timestamp, food category, estimated weight, and receiving partner name.

## 2. Eligible Food Categories for Redistribution
- Unserved Prepared Hot Foods: Must have maintained an internal holding temperature of >= 135 deg F (57 deg C) throughout service. Must be rapidly chilled to <= 41 deg F (5 deg C) within 4 hours using blast chillers or shallow pans before transfer.
- Uncut Whole Produce & Packaged Perishables: Apples, oranges, bananas, and commercially sealed dairy/salads are eligible for direct pantry transfer within manufacturer code dates.
- Ineligible Items: Previously served buffet items exposed to self-service student handling, reheated foods that have undergone multiple temperature cycles, and items held in the danger zone (41-135 deg F) exceeding 2 hours.

## 3. Campus Food Pantry & Community Partner Handoff
- When surplus food exceeds 15 kg for a single service period, the dining manager must issue an alert to the Campus Mutual Aid Pantry via the automated coordination channel.
- Pickup or temperature-controlled refrigerated transfer must occur within 24 hours of meal conclusion.
